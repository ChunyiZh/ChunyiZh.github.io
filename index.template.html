01 / Research研究方向

Chemical physics of solutions & interfaces溶液与界面化学物理

Based on the fundamental principles of statistical physics and quantum mechanics, we combine machine learning with first-principles approaches to study complex physicochemical behavior of matter at the atomic scale, focusing on the structure, dynamics and reaction processes of water, electrolytes and solid–liquid interfaces. By developing generalizable multiscale theoretical frameworks, we aim to reveal the fundamental laws of matter and improve predictions of the properties of complex condensed matter systems.基于统计物理与量子力学的基本原理，结合机器学习方法，从第一性原理出发研究原子尺度下物质复杂的物理化学行为，重点关注水、电解质以及固液界面的结构、动力学与反应过程。通过构建可泛化的多尺度理论框架，旨在揭示物质的基本规律并提升对复杂凝聚态体系性质的预测能力。
